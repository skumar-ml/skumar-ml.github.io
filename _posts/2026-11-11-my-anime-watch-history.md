I've cataloged what I've watched below, in roughly chronological order{% include academic-project/footnote.html text="I initially tried doing this from pure recall, then I realized I could just go through my Crunchyroll and Netflix watch history." %}. Below that is my top-10 ranking, a few other opinionated lists, and reviews on each show I've watched. I've included Western animated shows, even if they are technically not anime.

## Reflecting on my Anime Watching Experience

After compiling my watch history and the opinionated lists, I naturally wanted to reflect on my anime watch history.

**I love shonen.** It's not really a surprise. Most of my top 10 is shonen, and I especially love shonen with darker / adult themes (e.g., JJK, Cyberpunk). More philisophically, I feel that anime as a medium enables unique commentaries on pervasive themes like friendship, morality, life, etc, and shonen amplifies this through it's fantastical and sometimes intense setups (e.g., constant fights for life and death, mind-bending powers, fake worlds). Achieving this setup is orders of magnitude harder in real-world media, which sometimes leads to stale explorations of the same themes.

But *I don't like shonen for shonen's sake*. Great examples of this are Record of Ragnorok & Fate/Zero. I think the backdrop & emotional investment in a fight amplifies the "awe" of the fight itself. A crude analogy is a one-night stand vs. a meaningful relationship. Record of Ragnorok is like the one-night stand of shonen. 😂

The last point I'll make here is that *I love Freiren.* Freiren is technically a shonen, but it has so many other elements (slice of life, coming of age, fantasy, adventure) that are artfully combined. I really think it's a genre-breaking anime---at least, I've never seen anything like it.

**F*ck isekai.** I've watched a bunch of isekai now. I keep giving this genre a chance, mainly because it has shonen components and there's always some new isekai out whenever it's a dry season. The only isekai I've really liked is Shangri-La Frontier.

(function () {
  var input = document.getElementById('anime-review-search');
  var list = document.getElementById('anime-reviews');
  if (!input || !list) return;

  var reviews = Array.prototype.slice.call(list.querySelectorAll('.anime-review'));
  var empty = list.querySelector('.anime-review-empty');

  function filterReviews() {
    var query = input.value.trim().toLowerCase();
    var shown = 0;
    var first = true;

    reviews.forEach(function (review) {
      var titleEl = review.querySelector('.anime-review-title');
      var title = titleEl ? titleEl.textContent.toLowerCase() : '';
      var match = query === '' || title.indexOf(query) !== -1;
      review.hidden = !match;
      review.classList.toggle('is-first-match', match && first);
      if (match) {
        shown += 1;
        first = false;
      }
    });

    if (empty) empty.hidden = shown !== 0;
  }

  input.addEventListener('input', filterReviews);
})();
</script>
